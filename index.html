automated_vid

A personal, non-commercial tool that makes short videos in code (physics, maths and history simulations) and publishes them to its owner's own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Renders simulation videos on its owner's computers.
  • Uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels, with titles, descriptions, playlists and scheduled publish times, through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads view and watch-time reports for those same channels through the YouTube Analytics API, to decide what to make next.

Who can use it

Only its owner, signed in to their own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it never signs in to anyone else's account.
